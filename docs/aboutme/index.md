## About Me:

### Jason Corum

Ever since I was a young child I have been fascinated by the innerworkings of the everchanging world and the creations humanity has used to adapt to such changes. Through introductory classes to blacksmithing, welding, and woodcarving during boy scouts, hobbyist woodworking and plumbing alongside my maternal grandfather, and my introduction to computer aided drafting during high school I slowly developed my understanding through increasingly complex interactions with the world of engineering. After obtaining my Associates in Engineering from Wake Technical Community College I worked as overnight staff for one of Absolute Dental's 3D printing facilities until I was accepted into the William States Lee College of Engineering in the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. While at Charlotte I have developed a significant interest in the applications of thermodynamics and its influence on engineering design and development. I believe that the Engineering Discipline plays a fundamental role as the backbone of modern society, and that all engineers should strive for improvement in the quality of life for all creatures in our world.

My fascination in mechanical mechanical engineering has substantial focus around the relationships between the physical behavior of mechanical systems and their environment. I derive joy from developing a successful understanding of the interactions between the individual components of a larger system and how such interactions are influenced by geometry, materials, manufacturing and fabrication methods, along with the surrounding environmental factors and physical phenomenon. During my coursework I am continuing to develop skills in computer aided design, technical communication, engineering analysis, and fabrication methodologies. I am becoming an engineer with a high regard for practical solutions supported with documented reasoning and obtained through both quantitative and qualitative analysis. As my development into a prospective mechanical engineer progresses, I will continue honing my skills in the evaluation of competitive design alternatives, recognizing relevant design constraints, and making of design choices that can be justified through engineering principles and engineering standards.
